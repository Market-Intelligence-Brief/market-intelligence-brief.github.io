Market Thesis · U.S. Equities · 1–2 months: U.S. equities are more likely to decline than gain over 1–2 months as inflation and financing sensitivity pressure valuations. AI infrastructure demand remains an offset, not proof of broad-market resilience.

Why This Confidence: Inflation persistence and continued cash-market weakness support a negative 1–2 month outcome. Strong foundry demand, resilient employment and lower yields are material offsets; one intraday session does not establish persistence.

Thesis Evidence & Invalidation

Supporting Evidence

  • Producer inflation remains elevated after yesterday’s CPI shock. · BLS · U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics
  • Delayed cash snapshots show broad weakness, with technology and semiconductor losses larger than SPY’s. · Web finance quote feed

Opposing Evidence

  • TSMC’s strong outlook supports AI infrastructure demand. · TSMC
  • Lower long yields and low claims oppose an immediate broad earnings downturn. · MarketWatch / FactSet · U.S. Department of Labor

Would Invalidate This Outlook

  • Verified AI order cuts, tightening credit or persistent broad weakness would strengthen the negative case.
